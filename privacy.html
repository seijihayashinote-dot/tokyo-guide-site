Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This page explains what information is collected on this site, how it's used, and who it's shared with. This site is run by an individual tour guide (Seiji), not a company, so this policy is written simply and in plain language.

Information You Provide

If you submit a review through the review form, we collect:

  • Your name (or however you choose to identify yourself)
  • Country
  • Age group and group size
  • Your star rating and review text

This information is submitted voluntarily and is displayed publicly on the site as part of the reviews section. Please don't include personal details in your review text that you wouldn't want shown publicly.

Information Collected Automatically

This site uses Vercel Web Analytics to understand overall traffic (e.g. how many people visit, which pages are popular). This is privacy-friendly analytics — it does not use cookies and does not track you individually across sites.

To prevent spam submissions, the review form temporarily stores a one-way hashed version of your IP address (not the IP address itself) to apply basic rate limits. This hash cannot be reversed to identify you and is only used for spam prevention.

Third-Party Services

This site uses a small number of third-party services to function:

  • Google Calendar — used only to display publicly available tour dates. It does not collect any information about site visitors.
  • Instagram — booking inquiries are sent via Instagram Direct Message. Once you click through to Instagram, Instagram's own privacy policy applies to that conversation.
  • Vercel — the hosting provider for this site and its analytics.

Data Storage & Retention

Reviews are stored in a database until removed. You can request that your review be edited or removed at any time by messaging @seiji.guides.tokyo on Instagram.

Your Choices

You're welcome to contact us at any time to ask what information we have, or to request a correction or deletion. The fastest way to reach us is Instagram DM.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Message @seiji.guides.tokyo on Instagram.
